TouchGate

Privacy Policy

Effective and updated: 2026-11-26
Developer: Wang xiaohu

This policy applies to TouchGate (碰草门禁), an iOS app provided by Wang xiaohu, including its Widgets, Live Activities, and Apple Screen Time monitoring and shield extensions. It explains how the app uses Screen Time permissions, on-device camera vision checks, step and motion sensors, NFC tag reading, and local notifications strictly on your device, and how you can manage or delete your data.

No account is required and 100% of processing runs strictly on your iPhone. Your app shield selections, camera pose and outdoor nature verification, step counts, bound NFC/barcode anchors, challenge history, and optional local grass specimen photos stay exclusively on your device. The developer operates no user servers, collects or sells zero personal data, and includes zero ads or cross-app tracking.

1. On-device preferences, logs, and extension sync

Your gate rules (exercise type, rep or step targets, temporary pass duration, escalating friction, bedtime/work schedules, emergency skip count), challenge logs, achievement unlock progress, and bound physical anchor labels are stored exclusively on your device and in its Apple App Group container so the main app, Widgets, Live Activities, and Screen Time extensions stay synchronized.

TouchGate has no user accounts or cloud backend; your data never passes through developer servers.

2. Apple Screen Time (FamilyControls) access

Only after you explicitly authorize Apple Screen Time (FamilyControls, ManagedSettings, and DeviceActivity) and select apps or categories does TouchGate apply local shields, grant temporary passes after you finish a physical challenge, and automatically re-lock when your pass expires.

Per Apple’s privacy-preserving architecture, selected apps are stored solely as opaque system tokens on-device. The developer cannot read your installed app list or Screen Time analytics, and no Screen Time data is ever transmitted externally. You can revoke permission anytime in iOS Settings → Screen Time.

3. On-device camera, outdoor specimen photos, and anti-spoof checks

When you start an Exercise challenge (squats, jumping jacks, or high knees), an Outdoor Touch Grass challenge (live daylight grass/sky verification), or a Barcode scan, camera frames are analyzed strictly in volatile memory on your iPhone using Apple Vision. Video is never recorded or uploaded to any server.

When you pass an Outdoor Touch Grass challenge, the app may save a compressed local snapshot inside its private sandbox solely to display in your Specimen Wall and optional Touch Grass Proof share card. You can delete all local records and saved specimen photos anytime via Rules → Clear Local History.

4. Motion & Fitness (steps and micro-motion) and NFC tags

During a Stand & Walk challenge or Outdoor anti-spoof verification, TouchGate uses Apple CoreMotion (CMPedometer and CMMotionManager) strictly while the challenge is active to count live steps and verify natural handheld micro-motion. Collection stops immediately when the challenge ends, and no sensor data leaves your device.

When you bind or tap a physical NFC sticker, TouchGate reads the tag identifier locally via Apple CoreNFC solely to match against your own local anchor list.

5. Local notifications and shareable cards

Optional schedule reminders and shield prompt notifications are generated locally via UNUserNotificationCenter with no remote push server or push token collection.

When you choose to share an Achievement Card, Weekly Discipline Receipt, or Touch Grass Proof Card, the image is rendered on-device and handed to the standard iOS Share Sheet only upon your action.

6. In-App Purchase (TouchGate Pro Lifetime) and restoration

Core physical app shielding, Squats, Stand & Walk, Outdoor Touch Grass, 7-day reports, and Widgets are free. TouchGate offers an optional one-time non-consumable purchase (TouchGate Pro Lifetime) that unlocks daily escalating friction, Rule Tamper Lock, App Removal Protection, Bedtime and Work Focus auto-schedules, additional exercise modes, and high-res Epic/Legendary card exports.

All payments, receipt verifications, restorations, and refund/revocation updates are handled exclusively by Apple App Store / StoreKit 2. The developer never receives payment card details or Apple Account credentials. Apple may process commerce transactions and provide the developer with transaction notifications and aggregated sales reports under Apple’s privacy policy: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/.

7. Third-party SDKs, data deletion, and contact

TouchGate integrates no advertising SDKs, behavioral analytics trackers, or cross-app tracking frameworks, and never sells personal data. Apple may provide aggregated crash and performance diagnostics based on your device settings. This privacy page is hosted on GitHub Pages with no ads, scripts, or cookies set by us; standard connection logs are governed by GitHub’s privacy statement: https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement.

You can clear all local history and specimen photos inside the app at any time, and uninstalling the app permanently removes all local data. For privacy questions or support, contact Wang xiaohu via https://ai-topic.cn and mention TouchGate.
