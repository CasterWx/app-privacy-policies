Privacy Policy

Effective and updated: 2026-11-25
Developer: Wang xiaohu

This policy applies to FramePure (素框 FramePure), an iOS and iPadOS app provided by Wang xiaohu, including its Photos Share Extension and Apple Shortcuts actions. It explains how the app processes photos and EXIF metadata on your device, how optional city-name lookup and iCloud preset sync work, how in-app purchases are handled, and how you can manage your information.

No account is required. All photo decoding, EXIF parsing, framing, and GPS metadata scrubbing run 100% locally on your device. The developer operates no photo servers, does not upload or sell your photos, and includes zero ads or cross-app tracking.

1. Photo access and on-device rendering

When you choose photos or Live Photos via the system photo picker, drag and drop, the Photos Share Extension, or Apple Shortcuts (including JPEG, HEIC, and RAW/DNG), FramePure reads image pixels, Display P3 wide-gamut profiles, Apple HDR Gain Maps, and EXIF/TIFF optical parameters strictly on your device to preview and render framed exports.

All rendering executes locally in device memory and temporary storage. Your photos are never uploaded to a developer server. When you save an export, the app writes the finished image to your Photos library under system permission and clears temporary working files.

2. GPS scrubbing and optional safe city-name lookup

With GPS privacy scrubbing enabled by default, FramePure inspects and strips exact GPS latitude/longitude coordinates, camera and lens serial numbers, owner names, and proprietary MakerNotes from exported files so sharing photography online does not leak your home address or hardware identifiers.

FramePure never requests live device location permissions and does not track your movements. Only if you explicitly tap the safe city-name caption action inside the EXIF editor does the app pass the selected photo’s embedded EXIF coordinates to Apple’s CoreLocation reverse-geocoding service to retrieve a city-level name for your caption. Coordinates are never sent to the developer or stored as location history. Apple’s privacy policy governs its geocoding service: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/.

3. Local preferences, signatures and personal iCloud sync

FramePure stores your favorite frame templates, active framing layout, saved presets, and any handwritten transparent signature image locally and inside the app’s shared App Group container so the main app and Share Extension stay in sync.

The app uses Apple’s personal iCloud key-value storage (NSUbiquitousKeyValueStore) to sync your saved frame presets across your own iPhone, iPad, and Mac. This data resides exclusively in your personal Apple iCloud account and is inaccessible to the developer. You can disable iCloud sync in iOS Settings or delete saved presets and signatures inside the app at any time.

4. Purchases and restoration

The free tier includes 3 classic white gallery frames, lossless single-photo export, and full GPS metadata scrubbing with zero ads. One non-consumable lifetime in-app purchase (FramePure Pro) unlocks all 30 templates, 9-grid batch export, Live Photo framing, diptych/contact sheet layouts, editorial typography, and handwritten signature stamps.

All payment processing, entitlement verification, restoration, and revocation checks are handled by Apple App Store / StoreKit 2. The developer never receives payment card details or your Apple Account password. Apple’s privacy policy applies to its commerce services: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/.

5. User-initiated sharing and third-party services

When you share an exported image through the system share sheet to another app or AirDrop, the destination app’s own privacy policy governs how it handles the shared file.

FramePure integrates no advertising networks, behavioral analytics SDKs, or cross-app tracking tools, and never sells personal data. Apple may provide aggregated crash and performance diagnostics according to your device settings. This policy page is hosted on GitHub Pages with no ads, scripts, or cookies set by us; GitHub may process standard server logs under its privacy statement: https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement.

6. Retention, deletion and contact

Presets, favorites, and signature images remain in your device storage and personal iCloud storage until you delete them in the app or uninstall the app from iOS. The developer keeps no server-side copies and cannot remotely access or recover local data.

If you contact support, we use only the details you voluntarily provide to respond to your inquiry or privacy request. Please do not send private original photos, passwords, or payment credentials. Contact Wang xiaohu via https://ai-topic.cn and reference FramePure.
