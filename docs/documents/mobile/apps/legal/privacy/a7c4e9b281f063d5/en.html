Privacy Policy

Effective and updated: 2026-11-26
Developer: Wang xiaohu

This policy applies to PurrTouch (including its Home Screen and Lock Screen widget extension, PurrTouchWidgets), an iOS and iPadOS app provided by Wang xiaohu. It explains how local preferences and Companion Journal records are stored, how on-device motion sensors and audio are used, how version checks work, and how you control your data.

No account required; core petting and sleep features run 100% locally on your device. PurrTouch never requests Camera, Photos, Location, Microphone, or Contacts permissions. All nicknames, Companion Journal stats, and settings stay solely on your device with zero ads, zero analytics SDKs, and zero cross-app tracking.

1. Local preferences and Companion Journal

To remember your favorite setup and quiet companion history, PurrTouch stores your selected cat breed, visual style, viewpoint, ambience theme, body proportions, sensory and sleep settings, custom cat nicknames, daily and cumulative petting/purring durations, stroke distance, zone preferences, and unlocked milestones locally in on-device UserDefaults.

When you add Home Screen or Lock Screen widgets, the app shares a lightweight summary snapshot (selected breed, custom nickname, bond stage, personality tag, today’s calm duration, and 7-day rhythm) exclusively between PurrTouch and PurrTouchWidgets through Apple’s private App Group container (group.com.xiaohuwang.purrtouch). None of this data leaves your device.

2. Device motion sensor, audio, and haptics

When you choose the 3D Plush visual style, PurrTouch reads real-time CoreMotion gravity and tilt orientation strictly on-device to render subtle parallax depth. Motion values are processed transiently in memory and are never recorded, stored, or transmitted.

PurrTouch uses AVAudioEngine and Core Haptics locally to play bundled public-domain feline audio samples, synthesize window soundscapes, and drive tactile feedback. When the Sleep Fade-Out Timer is active, background audio playback is used solely to keep purrs and ambient soundscapes playing smoothly while locked until fade-out completes. The app never requests microphone permission and never records audio.

3. Optional App Store version check and native ratings

To let you know when an update is available, PurrTouch may query Apple’s public iTunes Lookup endpoint (itunes.apple.com/lookup) at most once every 24 hours—or whenever you manually tap Check for Updates—using only the app’s Bundle ID and region code. No personal information, device identifiers, or journal data are transmitted, and the check fails silently offline.

After natural usage milestones, the app may invoke Apple’s native StoreKit review prompt (AppStore.requestReview), which is handled directly by Apple under Apple’s Privacy Policy: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/.

4. No ads, no third-party tracking, and page hosting

PurrTouch includes no ads, no third-party analytics SDKs, and no cross-app tracking frameworks, and never sells or shares personal data. Apple may share anonymized, aggregated crash and performance diagnostics based on your iOS system settings.

This privacy policy page is hosted on GitHub Pages without ads, analytics scripts, or cookies set by us. GitHub may process standard server request logs under its privacy statement: https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement.

5. Retention, deletion, and contact

All preferences, custom cat names, Companion Journal entries, and widget snapshots remain solely on your device. Uninstalling PurrTouch from iOS or iPadOS removes all local app data permanently. The developer maintains no server-side copies and cannot remotely access or recover local data.

For privacy questions or support requests, contact Wang xiaohu via https://ai-topic.cn and reference PurrTouch. Please do not include sensitive personal credentials in support inquiries.
