Privacy Policy

Effective and updated: 2026-11-26
Developer: Wang xiaohu

This policy applies to AfterCaff (余因 AfterCaff), an iOS, iPadOS, and watchOS app provided by Wang xiaohu, including its Widgets, Live Activities, Control Center controls, Siri Shortcuts, and Apple Watch companion app. It explains how the app stores caffeine logs and metabolism settings locally, how optional two-way Apple HealthKit sync, on-device Vision OCR receipt scanning, personal iCloud sync, and in-app purchases work, and how you can manage or delete your data.

No account is required. Your caffeine intake logs, metabolism parameters, custom drink recipes, morning sleep check-ins, and receipt OCR scans are processed locally on your device and synced only via your private Apple iCloud key-value storage. The developer operates no user health servers, never collects or sells your health data, and includes zero ads or cross-app tracking.

1. On-device logs, settings, and companion sync

Drink logs (name, caffeine mg, time consumed, cup size, slow-sip duration, drink price, and subjective feeling tags), morning sleep check-ins, custom drink recipes, and metabolism preferences (half-life, sleep-safe threshold, daily limit, target bedtime, and gentle taper settings) are stored locally in SwiftData and your app’s shared App Group container so the main app, Widgets, Live Activities, Control Center, and Siri Shortcuts stay synchronized.

When paired with Apple Watch, AfterCaff transfers recent caffeine snapshots and wrist quick-logs directly between your iPhone and Apple Watch via Apple WatchConnectivity without passing through developer servers.

2. Apple HealthKit sleep stages and dietary caffeine

Only after you explicitly grant permission does AfterCaff read recent Apple HealthKit Sleep Analysis samples (Deep, REM, and Core sleep stages) to correlate bedtime caffeine with sleep quality locally on your device, and write logged caffeine amounts to Apple Health Dietary Caffeine.

AfterCaff strictly adheres to Apple HealthKit privacy guidelines: HealthKit data stays exclusively on your device, is never uploaded to external servers, is never used for advertising, marketing, or data brokering, and is never shared with third parties. You may revoke or adjust read/write permissions anytime in iOS Settings → Health → Data Access & Devices; declining permission does not block other app features.

3. On-device Vision OCR, camera, and photos access

When you scan a coffee receipt or cup label with the camera, pick an order screenshot via the system photo picker, or tap Scan Clipboard Screenshot, all text recognition runs 100% offline using Apple’s on-device Vision OCR engine. No images or recognized text are uploaded to external servers, and original images are not retained.

When you save a Weekly Flavor Poster to your Photos library, the app uses add-only photo library permission strictly to write the exported poster and never scans or reads your existing photos.

4. Personal iCloud sync and local reminders

AfterCaff uses Apple’s personal iCloud key-value storage (NSUbiquitousKeyValueStore) to sync recent drink logs, metabolism settings, custom recipes, pinned drinks, and sleep check-ins across your own devices signed into the same Apple Account. This data lives exclusively in your personal iCloud storage and cannot be accessed by the developer. You can disable iCloud sync anytime in system settings.

Optional daily Last Cup Cutoff reminders are scheduled strictly on-device via iOS UNUserNotificationCenter with no remote push server or device push token collection.

5. Subscriptions, lifetime purchase, and restoration

Core real-time metabolism curves, the 116+ calibrated beverage catalog, Dual-Cup PK simulation, offline OCR, 7-day reports, and Apple Health sync are free. AfterCaff Pro offers an auto-renewing Annual subscription and a one-time Lifetime non-consumable purchase to unlock 30-day analytics, unlimited custom recipes, all magazine poster themes, and 4 minimalist app icons.

All payments, renewals, restorations, and refund/revocation verifications are handled by Apple App Store / StoreKit 2. The developer never receives payment card details or Apple Account credentials. Apple’s privacy policy applies to its commerce services: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/.

6. Third-party SDKs, wellness disclaimer, and data control

AfterCaff integrates no advertising SDKs, behavioral analytics trackers, or cross-app tracking frameworks, and never sells personal data. Apple may provide aggregated crash and performance diagnostics based on your device settings. This policy page is hosted on GitHub Pages with no ads, scripts, or cookies set by us; standard connection logs are governed by GitHub’s privacy statement: https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement.

Metabolism curves and sleep-safe estimates are calculated using a public one-compartment Bateman model and reference caffeine values for wellness tracking only. AfterCaff is not a medical device and does not provide medical diagnosis or clinical advice. Consult a qualified physician if pregnant, nursing, or sensitive to caffeine.

You can export your history to CSV or delete individual entries and custom recipes inside the app at any time, and uninstalling removes all local device data. For privacy questions or requests, contact Wang xiaohu via https://ai-topic.cn and reference AfterCaff (please do not send sensitive medical records or payment credentials).
