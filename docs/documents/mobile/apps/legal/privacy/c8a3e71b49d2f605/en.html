Privacy Policy

Effective and updated: 2026-11-25
Developer: Wang xiaohu

This policy applies to BodyTrace (体迹 BodyTrace), an iOS app provided by Wang xiaohu, including its Apple Shortcuts actions. It explains how the app stores your symptom records and photos locally on your device, how optional read-only Apple HealthKit sleep correlation and live barometric weather lookups work, how in-app purchases are handled, and how you can export or delete your data.

No account is required. All symptom logs, VAS intensity scores, triggers, medications, rash photos, and consultation report headers remain strictly on your iPhone in local SwiftData storage. The developer operates no health-data servers, never uploads or sells your personal health data, and includes zero ads or cross-app tracking.

1. On-device health records and photos

All symptom categories, VAS intensity scores (1–10), onset timestamps and durations, suspected triggers, acute medications and relief ratings, notes, daily calm check-ins, custom trigger tags, and optional report header fields are stored exclusively on your device in local SwiftData and UserDefaults storage and are never transmitted to developer servers.

When you attach a rash or symptom reference photo via the iOS system photo picker (PhotosPicker), BodyTrace accesses only the specific image you select and stores it inside your local app database. It does not scan your photo library or upload photos to any external server.

2. Apple HealthKit sleep data (read-only, on-device)

Only after you explicitly grant permission does BodyTrace request read-only access to Apple HealthKit Sleep Analysis samples to calculate your personal sleep baseline and prior-night sleep duration for display on the Today dashboard, Insights view, and your exported A4 Doctor Summary PDF.

BodyTrace strictly follows Apple HealthKit privacy guidelines: HealthKit data stays exclusively on your device, is never uploaded to the cloud, is never used for advertising, marketing, or data brokering, and is never shared with third parties. You may revoke HealthKit access anytime in iOS Settings → Health → Data Access & Devices, or enter prior-night sleep manually.

3. Location and live barometric weather lookup

Only when you grant 'While Using the App' location permission does BodyTrace use your current approximate coordinates to request local barometric pressure, 12-hour pressure trend, temperature, and humidity from Apple WeatherKit to help surface weather-related flare triggers. If WeatherKit is temporarily unavailable, the app falls back to the public Open-Meteo forecast API for the same real-time weather metrics.

Coordinates are used solely for the immediate weather lookup. BodyTrace never tracks, uploads, or maintains a location history; only the resulting weather values attached to a symptom entry you save are stored locally. Apple Weather is governed by Apple’s Privacy Policy (https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/) and WeatherKit attribution notice (https://weatherkit.apple.com/legal-attribution.html); Open-Meteo is governed by its terms (https://open-meteo.com/en/terms).

4. Local reminders, Shortcuts, and user-initiated exports

Optional evening check-in reminders (21:30) and local barometric drop alerts are scheduled exclusively on-device via iOS UNUserNotificationCenter without remote push servers. Apple Shortcuts and Siri voice actions also run locally on your device.

Bilingual A4 clinical PDF reports, CSV tables, and JSON backups are generated locally on your iPhone and leave the app only when you explicitly share or print them via the iOS system share sheet or AirPrint. Any recipient you choose handles shared files under its own privacy terms.

5. Subscriptions, lifetime purchase, and restoration

Core symptom logging, daily calm check-ins, and 14/30-day insights are free. BodyTrace Pro offers Monthly and Annual auto-renewing subscriptions (which may include a 7-day free trial) as well as a one-time Lifetime non-consumable purchase to unlock bilingual A4 PDF export, 90-day and all-time analytics, local barometric alerts, and full backup/restore.

All billing, subscription management, restoration, and revocation verification are processed by Apple App Store / StoreKit 2. The developer never receives your payment card details or Apple Account credentials. Apple’s privacy policy applies to its commerce services: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/.

6. Third-party SDKs, medical disclaimer, and deletion

BodyTrace includes no third-party advertising networks, behavioral analytics SDKs, or cross-app tracking tools, and never sells personal data. Apple may provide aggregated crash and performance diagnostics according to your device settings. This policy page is hosted on GitHub Pages with no ads, scripts, or cookies set by us; GitHub may process standard server logs under its privacy statement: https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement.

BodyTrace is a personal symptom journal and consultation-preparation aid, not a medical device, and does not provide medical diagnosis or treatment advice. You can export a full JSON backup or permanently erase all local records anytime under Settings → Data & Backup, or remove all local data by uninstalling the app. For privacy inquiries, contact Wang xiaohu via https://ai-topic.cn and reference BodyTrace (please do not send sensitive medical files or payment credentials).
