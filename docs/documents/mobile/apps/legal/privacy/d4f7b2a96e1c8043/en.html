Privacy Policy

Effective and updated: 2026-11-26
Developer: Wang xiaohu

This policy applies to LifeWeeks (每周一格 LifeWeeks), an iOS and iPadOS app provided by Wang xiaohu, including its Lock Screen and Home Screen widgets and Apple Shortcuts actions. It explains how your weekly journal entries, photos, and life chronicle books are stored on-device and optionally in your private iCloud database, how system permissions work strictly on-device, and how you can export or permanently delete your data at any time.

No account is required. Your birth date, life books, weekly reflections, mood colors, time capsules, chapters, and photos remain strictly on your device and in your personal Apple ID private iCloud database. The developer operates no content servers, never reads or sells your journal data, and includes zero third-party ads, analytics, or tracking SDKs.

1. On-device storage and private iCloud sync

All data you enter—including your birth date, lifespan grid span, custom chronicle books, weekly headlines and notes, moods, location labels, life chapters, time capsules, and compressed photos—is stored locally on your device in SwiftData and UserDefaults. A lightweight summary snapshot is shared via an App Group solely with the app’s own WidgetKit extensions.

When iCloud sync is enabled on a device signed into your Apple Account, Apple CloudKit synchronizes your entries across your devices using your private iCloud database. This private database is controlled solely by your Apple Account; the developer cannot access, query, or decrypt your entries. Apple’s Privacy Policy governs iCloud services: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/.

2. Photos, Microphone, Speech Recognition, and Biometrics

Photo Library access is used strictly on-device when you choose photos for a week or run Photo Time Machine to cluster past weeks locally; Add-Only Photo Library permission is used solely when you save a 3× share poster to Photos. Your photo library is never scanned in the background or uploaded to developer servers.

Microphone and Speech Recognition permissions are requested only when you start the 30-second Voice Memo reflection to transcribe speech into text using Apple’s Speech framework. Audio recordings are not saved or uploaded to developer servers. Face ID / Touch ID Privacy Lock relies exclusively on Apple’s LocalAuthentication framework on-device; biometric data is never accessed or stored by the app.

3. On-device insights, local reminders, and Shortcuts

Annual keyword extraction and mood analytics run 100% on-device via Apple’s NaturalLanguage framework with no external AI API calls.

Optional weekly gentle reminders and Time Capsule unlock notifications are scheduled locally through iOS UNUserNotificationCenter without remote push servers. Siri Shortcuts and App Intents execute locally on your device.

4. Data sovereignty and user-initiated exports

You can export your complete archive as an open-format Markdown + Photos (.zip) backup anytime under Settings → Backup, Export & Demo, or generate printable vector PDF chronicles and 3× art-print posters. All exports are rendered locally on your device and leave the app only when you explicitly share or save them via the iOS share sheet; any destination you choose is governed by its own privacy terms.

5. Subscriptions, lifetime buyout, and restoration

The full 4,000-week grid, weekly journaling, single-photo entries, and complete Markdown ZIP backup/restore are free forever. LifeWeeks Pro offers a one-time Lifetime non-consumable purchase and an Annual auto-renewing subscription to unlock up to 4 photos per week, unlimited life chapters, multiple chronicle books, curated paper themes, and printable vector PDF chronicles.

All billing, renewal management, Family Sharing, purchase restoration, and revocation checks are handled by Apple App Store / StoreKit 2. The developer never receives your payment card details or Apple Account credentials. Apple’s Privacy Policy applies to its commerce services: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/.

6. Zero third-party tracking, deletion, and contact

LifeWeeks contains no third-party advertising networks, behavioral analytics SDKs, or cross-app tracking frameworks, and never sells personal data. Apple may share anonymized crash and performance diagnostics according to your device’s Analytics & Improvements settings. This policy page is hosted on GitHub Pages with no ads, scripts, or cookies set by us; GitHub may process standard security server logs under its privacy statement: https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement.

You can permanently erase all local entries and chapters anytime under Settings → Backup, Export & Demo → Clear All Local Records, or uninstall the app and manage your iCloud storage to remove synced copies. For privacy inquiries, contact Wang xiaohu via https://ai-topic.cn and reference LifeWeeks (please do not send personal journal backups or payment credentials).
