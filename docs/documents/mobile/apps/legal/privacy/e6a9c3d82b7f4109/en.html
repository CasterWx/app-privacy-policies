CleanRoll (清一清相册)

Privacy Policy

Effective and updated: 2026-11-26
Developer: Wang xiaohu

This policy applies to CleanRoll (清一清相册), an iOS and iPadOS app provided by Wang xiaohu, including its Home Screen Widget and Dynamic Island Live Activity extension. It explains how the app scans your Photos library and compresses videos or images locally on your device, how metadata and local caches are handled, how in-app purchases work, and how you can manage your information.

No account required. 100% on-device and offline. All similar-burst detection, blurry-photo screening, hardware video compression, image resizing, and Live Photo conversion run strictly on your device. The developer operates no media servers, never uploads or sells your photos or videos, and includes zero ads or cross-app tracking.

1. Photos library access and on-device analysis

With your system Photos permission, CleanRoll reads local photos, Live Photos, and videos strictly on your device (configured for local-only offline access without triggering developer server uploads) to detect exact duplicates, similar bursts, blurry shots, old screenshots, and oversized videos using Apple Vision, sharpness analysis, and local pixel digests.

Items you mark while swiping or reviewing are placed in an in-app Staging Vault first and are never silently deleted. Only when you tap the final cleanup action and confirm Apple’s system deletion dialog are selected items moved to iOS’s Recently Deleted album, where Apple keeps them recoverable for 30 days.

2. Video compression, image conversion and metadata privacy

CleanRoll’s compression studio uses Apple hardware VideoToolbox, AVFoundation, and ImageIO locally on your device for reverse-bitrate video compression, HDR-to-SDR tone mapping, 3-second split trial previews, exact target-size image conversion (HEIF, JPEG, PNG), and Live Photo exports. Temporary working files stay inside the app’s local sandbox and are cleaned up automatically.

CleanRoll never requests live device location permission and does not track your whereabouts. Location-grouped swipe decks match existing photo coordinates strictly against a built-in offline city table on your device with zero network geocoding requests. When compressing or exporting media, you may preserve original capture dates and locations or toggle GPS and EXIF stripping before sharing.

3. Local cache, App Group widget data and Live Activities

To keep scans fast and remember your progress, CleanRoll stores scan feature caches, whitelist and favorite-protection settings, staging and review state, and reclaimed-space totals locally on your device. Summary counts, reclaimable-space totals, and a single low-resolution local thumbnail are shared with your Home Screen Widget through the app’s private App Group container (group.com.xiaohuwang.cleanroll).

During video compression, CleanRoll uses Apple ActivityKit locally to display live encoding progress and speed on the Lock Screen and Dynamic Island without sending photos or file names to any external push server.

4. Purchases and restoration

CleanRoll provides a free trial cleanup allowance (up to 100 items or 500 MB) with zero ads and zero subscriptions. One non-consumable lifetime purchase (CleanRoll Pro, product ID: com.xiaohuwang.cleanroll.pro.lifetime) permanently unlocks unlimited cleanup and full studio features.

All payment processing, entitlement checks, purchase restoration, and refund handling are managed exclusively by Apple App Store / StoreKit 2. The developer never receives payment card details or your Apple Account password. Apple’s privacy policy governs its commerce services: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/.

5. User-initiated sharing and third-party services

When you choose to share a compressed video, photo, or cleanup summary poster via the system share sheet or AirDrop, the destination app’s privacy policy governs how it handles the shared file.

CleanRoll integrates no advertising networks, behavioral analytics SDKs, or cross-app tracking frameworks, and never sells personal data. Apple may provide aggregated crash and performance diagnostics according to your iOS settings. This privacy page is hosted on GitHub Pages with no ads, scripts, or cookies set by us; GitHub may process standard server logs under its privacy statement: https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement.

6. Retention, deletion and contact

All preferences, scan caches, and widget summaries stay solely on your device. You can clear the Staging Vault or whitelist inside the app anytime, or uninstall the app from iOS to remove all local app data. The developer keeps no server-side copies and cannot remotely access or recover local data.

For privacy questions or support requests, contact Wang xiaohu via https://ai-topic.cn and reference CleanRoll (清一清相册). Please do not send private original media, passwords, or payment credentials.
