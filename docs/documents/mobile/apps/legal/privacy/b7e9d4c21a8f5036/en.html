ScrollInk (偷得半页闲)

Privacy Policy

Effective and updated: 2026-11-26
Developer: Wang xiaohu

This policy applies to ScrollInk (偷得半页闲), an iOS and iPadOS local e-book reader app provided by Wang xiaohu, including its Share Extension and Home Screen Widgets. It explains how the app handles your imported books, custom fonts, reading progress, annotations, optional local Wi-Fi transfer, personal iCloud key-value sync, and in-app purchases.

No account is required and no online book store or scraper is included. All imported e-books (EPUB, TXT, Markdown, PDF) and custom fonts are stored and parsed 100% locally on your device. The developer operates no book or user servers, never uploads or sells your library or reading history, and includes zero ads or cross-app tracking.

1. Local books, custom fonts and Share Extension

When you import local e-books (EPUB, TXT, Markdown, PDF) or custom font files (TTF, OTF, TTC) via the Files picker, AirDrop, or the system Share Extension, ScrollInk copies, parses, and typesets those files strictly inside your device sandbox and shared App Group container.

All parsing, Chinese/English typesetting, speech synthesis (using Apple’s on-device AVSpeechSynthesizer), and offline EPUB fallback rendering execute locally. Your books and notes are never uploaded to any developer server.

2. Local Network Wi-Fi transfer

Only when you explicitly open the Wi-Fi Transfer screen does the app request iOS Local Network permission to start a temporary local HTTP server on your current Wi-Fi network so you can transfer books or fonts directly from a computer browser on the same LAN.

Transfer traffic stays strictly inside your local network and never traverses external developer servers. The local server stops immediately when you close the Wi-Fi Transfer screen.

3. Photo Library save and custom cover picker

ScrollInk requests Photo Library write-only permission solely when you tap Save in the Quote Card Studio to save an exported quote poster image to your Photos library.

When you choose a custom book cover through the system PhotosPicker, the app reads only the single image you explicitly selected and stores it locally.

4. Widgets, personal iCloud sync and JSON backups

ScrollInk stores reading preferences, bookmarks, highlights, reading time statistics, and a minimal Home Screen Widget snapshot locally and inside its shared App Group container.

The app uses Apple’s personal iCloud key-value storage (NSUbiquitousKeyValueStore) to sync reading progress (keyed by file hash) and your personal seal text across your own iPhone and iPad. This data lives exclusively in your personal Apple iCloud account and cannot be accessed by the developer. You may also export or restore a portable JSON backup of your reading progress and highlights manually at any time.

5. In-app purchases and restoration

ScrollInk offers an optional one-time non-consumable lifetime purchase (Pro) with no recurring subscriptions and no advertisements.

All payment processing, entitlement checks, and purchase restorations are handled by Apple App Store / StoreKit 2. The developer never receives payment card details or your Apple Account password. Apple’s privacy policy governs App Store commerce: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/.

6. Sharing, retention, deletion and contact

When you share exported quote cards, Markdown notes, or JSON backups via the iOS share sheet, the destination app’s privacy policy applies. ScrollInk integrates no advertising SDKs, analytics trackers, or cross-app tracking frameworks, and never sells personal data. This privacy page is hosted on GitHub Pages with no ads, scripts, or cookies set by us; standard hosting logs are governed by GitHub’s privacy statement: https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement.

Your library, fonts, highlights, and settings stay on your device and in your personal iCloud account until you delete them in the app or uninstall the app. The developer maintains no server-side copies and cannot recover local data.
