Starveil Theatre

Privacy Policy

Effective and updated: 2026-11-13
Developer: Wang xiaohu

This policy applies to Starveil Theatre (星幕剧场：小丑牌), an iOS and iPadOS app provided by Wang xiaohu. It explains what the game stores on your device, how purchases and sharing work, and how you can manage your information.

No developer account is required. Profiles, runs and Workshop designs are stored on your device. There is currently no developer cloud-save service, advertising or behavioral tracking. Apple handles purchases, and you choose when to share.

1. Local game data

The game stores profile names and local profile identifiers, progression and unlocks, active runs, records, Workshop designs and custom Joker abilities, and language, sound, haptic and appearance settings on your device. This enables continued play, separate profiles, creation and saved preferences. The game does not automatically upload this information to a developer server.

The app uses on-device storage and system preferences. Some settings-recovery operations keep a local copy of damaged settings for recovery; those copies are not automatically sent to the developer. There is currently no developer account system, online leaderboard or cross-device cloud-save service.

2. Purchases and restoration

The free download includes a repeatable trial. One non-consumable in-app purchase grants full-game access. Apple handles purchasing, payment and restoration through App Store / StoreKit services. The app reads and verifies purchase entitlements to grant access and handle purchase, restoration or revocation states.

The developer does not obtain payment card details or your Apple Account password through the game, and does not write payment receipts or transaction identifiers into player-profile saves. Apple may process account and purchase data under its own terms and provide sales and financial reports to the developer. Restoring a purchase restores an eligible entitlement, not profiles, runs or designs. Apple’s privacy policy applies to its services: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/.

3. Workshop import and sharing

Imported design codes are parsed on your device. Exporting or sharing requires your action. A code contains the current design’s name, custom card names, stages, seed, rules and ability parameters. It does not automatically include local player profiles, progression records or active runs.

You provide the text in your designs. If you put personal information in names or other text fields, that text will be included when you share. Check the content first. The system share sheet passes the selected content to your chosen app or recipient, whose own rules govern subsequent handling. The game has no public design community and does not automatically publish designs online.

4. Feedback and support

Opening Help or Feedback does not automatically send a report. You can enter a problem description and preview a summary containing the app version, build, operating system, sound and haptic settings, and trial or purchase-entitlement state. This summary and your description are passed to another app only after you choose a sharing method. Reports do not automatically attach save files, profile names, transaction identifiers, payment credentials or full logs.

If you contact the developer through support channels, we process the description, attachments and reply contact details you voluntarily provide to answer questions, investigate problems or handle privacy requests. Do not send passwords, full payment information or unrelated personal information. We retain this correspondence only as needed to handle the request, keep necessary support records or meet legal obligations, then delete it or remove personal information when no longer needed.

5. Third-party services, permissions and diagnostics

The current app does not integrate advertising, cross-app tracking or third-party behavioral analytics, and does not sell personal information. Core gameplay does not require contacts, camera, microphone or precise-location permissions.

Apple may process App Store, system diagnostic and analytics information according to your device settings and its policies, and make reports available to developers. The developer may use Apple-provided reports to understand reliability and performance. The game does not operate a separate remote crash-reporting service.

This policy website is hosted on GitHub Pages. It has no ads, analytics scripts, embedded third-party fonts or cookies set by us. When you visit, GitHub may process your IP address, browser information and request logs to operate and protect its service under its privacy statement: https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement. Visiting the support website or using another app to share content is subject to that service’s own privacy rules.

6. Retention, deletion and backups

Local game data remains in the app’s device storage for continued play and creation. You can delete unwanted designs in the Workshop. A run that has already started may keep its own snapshot of the rules.

Deleting the app through the operating system removes its on-device data; using Offload App may preserve documents and data. Whether system backups retain or restore this information depends on your Apple device and backup settings. The developer cannot recover local runs from a server or remotely delete data on your device. Export any Workshop designs you want to keep before deleting the app.

You can use the support website to request access to, correction or deletion of information you voluntarily sent to the developer, or exercise other rights available under applicable law. We may need to verify the connection between a request and the relevant information. Manage or request changes to information held by Apple, GitHub or a sharing recipient through that service.

7. Children and young players

The game does not require a name, contact details or date of birth to play. Young players should use purchases, sharing and external contact features with a parent or guardian’s guidance. If you believe a child has sent unnecessary personal information to the developer, contact support so it can be investigated and addressed.

8. Changes and contact

If online features, third-party services or data practices change, we will update this policy and its date, and provide additional notice or obtain consent where required by applicable law.

For questions about this app’s data practices or privacy requests, contact Wang xiaohu through https://ai-topic.cn and identify Starveil Theatre in your request. Do not post save files, purchase credentials or other personal information in public GitHub issues.
