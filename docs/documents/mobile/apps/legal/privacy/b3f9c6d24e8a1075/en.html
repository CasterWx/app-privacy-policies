Privacy Policy

Effective and updated: 2026-11-28
Developer: Wang xiaohu

This policy applies to StudioPulse (Bundle ID: com.xiaohuwang.StudioPulseApp), an iOS, iPadOS, and macOS app provided by Wang xiaohu, including its Home Screen and Lock Screen Widget extension. Based strictly on the app’s actual code and data flows, it explains how your App Store Connect credentials and analytics stay on-device, how the optional Zero-Retention Push Relay and Self-Hosted Hub mode work, how StoreKit 2 purchases are processed, and how you control your data.

Pure on-device BYOK (Bring Your Own Key) architecture with no developer account required. Your App Store Connect .p8 private keys, Key ID, Issuer ID, and Vendor Number are stored exclusively in your device’s system Keychain and are never uploaded to developer servers. All sales, subscription, ranking, and review caches stay on-device. The optional Real-Time Push Relay is zero-retention—it never accesses .p8 keys and never stores financial records. Zero ads, zero analytics SDKs, and zero cross-app tracking.

1. Pure On-Device BYOK Architecture and Local Storage

By default, StudioPulse operates in pure on-device direct-connect mode. Your App Store Connect API credentials (Key ID, Issuer ID, .p8 private key PEM, and Vendor Number), along with optional Relay management secrets or Self-Hosted Hub tokens, are stored exclusively in your device’s system Keychain (accessible only after first unlock on this device, isolated per studio profile) and are never uploaded to developer servers.

ES256 JWT signing is performed strictly on-device using Apple CryptoKit. Your device communicates directly with Apple’s official App Store Connect REST API (api.appstoreconnect.apple.com), Apple’s public iTunes Lookup, Search, and RSS Top 200 endpoints (itunes.apple.com), and a public currency exchange-rate endpoint (open.er-api.com, used solely for public multi-currency reference rates) without routing through developer servers.

Downloaded sales reports, subscription summaries and events, ranking histories, customer review caches, studio profiles, and widget snapshots remain strictly inside your device’s private app sandbox and Apple App Group container (group.com.xiaohuwang.studiopulse.app) to keep the main app and Widget extension synchronized. Optional background auto-patrol (BGAppRefreshTask) and local notifications (UNUserNotificationCenter) for review status changes, Top 200 movers, and low-star reviews run entirely on-device.

2. Optional Zero-Retention Push Relay

Real-Time Push Notifications are disabled by default and are only activated if you explicitly enable and provision a dedicated push route on the official Zero-Retention Push Relay (studiopulse-relay.studio-payment-hub.workers.dev, hosted on Cloudflare Workers and D1).

When enabled, the relay stores only the minimal routing configuration needed to deliver alerts: a randomly generated tenant route ID and unguessable webhook token, a SHA-256 hash of your management secret (never stored in plaintext), your display label, your optional APNs device token (when iOS push alerts are enabled), the outbound webhook URLs and optional signing secrets you configure (such as Feishu, DingTalk, WeCom, Bark, Slack, Discord, or a generic HTTPS webhook), plus basic operational counters (forward count, last event timestamp) and short-window IP/token rate-limit counters to prevent abuse.

The relay never requests or accesses your .p8 private keys and never persists financial transaction amounts, order IDs, customer details, or sales ledgers. When Apple sends an App Store Server Notifications V2 payload to your route, the relay verifies Apple’s JWS signature against Apple Root CA - G3 in memory, formats and forwards the alert to your configured channels or APNs, and discards the transaction payload immediately. You may rotate your webhook URL or permanently delete your cloud route (erasing the server routing record and local relay secrets) at any time from inside the app.

3. Optional Self-Hosted Hub Mode

If you explicitly choose Self-Hosted Hub mode in Settings, StudioPulse connects solely to the server URL and API token that you supply. Data processing on your self-hosted server is controlled entirely by your own deployment; the developer has no access to your self-hosted server or its data.

4. In-App Purchases and Subscriptions (StudioPulse Pro)

StudioPulse offers optional Pro plans (Monthly Subscription, Yearly Subscription, and Lifetime Buyout) via Apple StoreKit 2. All payment processing, receipt verification, purchase restoration, and refund or revocation synchronization are handled exclusively by Apple App Store. The developer never receives your payment card details or Apple Account credentials.

Apple processes transactions under Apple’s Privacy Policy and provides standard App Store Server Notifications V2 and aggregated sales reports to the developer (Wang xiaohu). See Apple’s Privacy Policy: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/.

5. Version Checks, Native Ratings, Zero Ads, and Zero Tracking

To notify you when a new version is available, StudioPulse may query Apple’s public iTunes Lookup endpoint (itunes.apple.com/lookup) at most once every 24 hours—or whenever you tap Check for Updates—using only the app’s Bundle ID and region code, with no personal identifiers or business data sent. After natural usage milestones, the app may invoke Apple’s native rating prompt (AppStore.requestReview), which is handled directly by Apple.

StudioPulse contains zero third-party advertising SDKs, zero analytics/telemetry SDKs, and zero cross-app tracking frameworks (NSPrivacyTracking = false), and never sells or shares personal data. Apple may provide anonymized, aggregated crash and performance diagnostics based on your device settings.

This privacy policy page is hosted on GitHub Pages without ads, analytics scripts, or cookies set by us. Standard server access logs are governed by GitHub’s privacy statement: https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement.

6. Data Retention, Deletion, and Contact

All local credentials, sales ledgers, ranking histories, review caches, and widget snapshots remain solely on your device. You can delete individual studio profiles, revoke and delete your optional cloud push route inside the app at any time, or uninstall StudioPulse to remove all local app data. Because the developer holds no server-side copies of your local keys or ledgers, we cannot access or recover them remotely.

For privacy questions or support, please contact Wang xiaohu via https://ai-topic.cn and reference StudioPulse. Never send .p8 private keys, management secrets, or sensitive credentials in support inquiries.
